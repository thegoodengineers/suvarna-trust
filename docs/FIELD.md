# Field interviews: gold given to karigars (2.2)

Goal by 12 Nov: at least 3 jewellers and 3 karigars. Go in pairs: one asks, one writes.

## Before each interview
- Say who you are: students building a tool for Tech4Bharat, not selling anything.
- Ask consent: "Can we note your answers? We won't use your name or shop name unless you say yes."
- Never ask for or photograph real customer details, bills or gold stock.
- Record answers, not your opinion. Write numbers exactly as said.

## Jeweller questions
1. How many karigars do you give gold to, and how many open orders right now?
2. When you issue gold, how do you record it, and who keeps the record?
3. What wastage percentage do you agree, and does it change by design?
4. In a typical month, how many grams can't be explained? How do you settle that?
5. What happened the last time a piece was late for a wedding?
6. How long after delivery do you usually pay the karigar, and why?
7. What would make you trust a phone record instead of your notebook?
8. Would you pay for this? Roughly how much per month would feel fair?

## Karigar questions
1. How many jewellers do you work for at once?
2. When you receive gold, do you keep your own record? How?
3. What do you do when the jeweller's number differs from yours?
4. How long do you wait to be paid after delivering?
5. Which language do you prefer? Do you use WhatsApp voice notes?
6. Would you trust a record the jeweller also controls? What would make it fair to you?
7. Have you ever needed a loan for your work? What did the lender ask for?

## Show the demo (last 5 minutes of every interview)
Open the demo on your phone (see README) and run the story: the 0.500 g gap, the missing scrap photo,
the re-weigh, both approving. Then ask:
1. What here would you never do in real life? What is confusing?
2. Karigars: press "सुनिए" (read it to me). Was it clear? Which language would you want?
3. Would you confirm the other person's entry on your phone? What would stop you?
Write their words exactly. Don't explain or defend the demo while they try it.
