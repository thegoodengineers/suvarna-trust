Why both sides trust it

Nothing can be quietly changed, and no one is paid without both saying yes.

Milligrams, not guesses

Whole milligrams in tested code. The AI never computes a balance.

A seal on every entry

Each entry carries the hash of the one before. Corrections are new entries.

Two approvals

Payment needs both sides to approve one exact settlement, nothing unexplained.

An agent that explains

Kannada, Hindi or English, without blame. It suggests; people decide.
