# Interview kit (print one page per interview)

Use with `docs/FIELD.md` (full question list and the notes table). Ten minutes, in pairs: one asks, one writes.
Write numbers exactly as said. No real names, shop names, customer details or photos of gold stock without a yes.

**Kannada lines: to be checked by a native speaker before printing.** Hindi lines: read aloud once before use.

## 1. Opening and consent (say this first, word for word)

| हिंदी | हम इंजीनियरिंग के छात्र हैं और Tech4Bharat नाम के हैकथॉन के लिए एक ऐप बना रहे हैं। हम कुछ बेच नहीं रहे। क्या हम आपसे दस मिनट कुछ सवाल पूछकर आपके जवाब लिख सकते हैं? आपकी हाँ के बिना हम आपका नाम या दुकान का नाम नहीं लिखेंगे। आप कभी भी रोक सकते हैं। |

Consent noted: ☐ answers ☐ quote with no name ☐ first name ☐ shop name ☐ photo of the demo being used (no gold, no faces)

## 2. Six questions (ask in their language; write the answer, not your opinion)

Jewellers: J1–J3 (then FIELD.md J4–J8 if there is time). Karigars: K1–K3 (then FIELD.md K4–K7).

## 3. Last five minutes: show the demo

Open the demo on a phone (`CONTRIBUTING.md`). Show the 0.500 g gap, press "सुनिए" / "ಓದಿ ಹೇಳಿ", re-weigh, approve.
Then ask: "What here would you never do in real life? What is confusing?" Don't explain or defend; write their words.

## 4. Close
Thank them. Ask: "Can we come back with the next version?" Write the date, role, area and language in FIELD.md the same day.

## Short form for karigars who can't meet (Google Form, or send as voice notes)

Form description: "Students' project for Tech4Bharat. Not selling anything. Your name is optional and will not be
published. You can answer by voice note on WhatsApp instead." Put each question in English, हिंदी and ಕನ್ನಡ.

1. You are: ○ karigar ○ jeweller ○ other ______
2. Town / market area (no shop name): ______
3. How many jewellers do you take gold from right now? ○ 1 ○ 2–3 ○ 4 or more
4. When you receive gold, how is it written down? ☐ jeweller's book ☐ my own book ☐ phone / WhatsApp ☐ nothing ☐ other
5. In the last 6 months, was there a difference in grams between your record and the jeweller's? ○ no ○ once ○ several times
6. If yes: roughly how many grams, and how was it settled? ______
7. After you deliver, how many days until you are paid? ○ same day ○ within a week ○ 1–4 weeks ○ more than a month
8. Which language do you want an app in? ☐ ಕನ್ನಡ ☐ हिंदी ☐ English ☐ other ______
9. Would you confirm a weight on your phone if the jeweller could not change it later? ○ yes ○ no ○ not sure. Why? ______
10. Can we call you for 10 minutes? Phone (optional): ______  ☐ I agree my answers can be used without my name.
